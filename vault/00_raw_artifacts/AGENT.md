# Raw Artifacts Signpost

Store source material or source records here before any interpretation. Do not overwrite original artifacts during processing.

Direct children:

- `text/` - direct text reflections and short written captures.
- `writing/` - long-form writing, essays, journals, and posts. Payload copies live in `writing/files/`; artifact notes live in `writing/notes/`.
- `images/` - photos and screenshots. Payload copies live in `images/files/`; artifact notes live in `images/notes/`.
- `projects/` - project records, READMEs, design docs, and related artifacts. Payload copies live in `projects/files/`; artifact notes live in `projects/notes/`.
